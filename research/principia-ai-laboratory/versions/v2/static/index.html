EXPERIMENTAL RIGOUR

How this study works

Operational definitions, controls, and the limits of the conclusions that may lawfully be drawn.

General mathematical relation

The disposition D, available appearances A, and corrective operations C determine judgement J and the next disposition. For a closed inference pathway, processing alone cannot increase information about the world beyond the information supplied through its inputs and acquired disposition.

Jₙ ∼ G(Dₙ, Aₙ)   ·   Dₙ₊₁ ∼ U(Dₙ, Aₙ, Cₙ)
I(W;J) ≤ I(W;D,A)

Prespecified experimental controls

All synthetic tasks supply an independently generated ground truth, seeded random generation, separate train/test samples, multiple learner architectures and random seeds, exact Bayes ceilings where possible, and paired comparisons. Quantities include accuracy, Brier score, log loss, calibration error, confidence-selective risk, total train operations (proxy), memory lookup rate and sensor queries.

The distinction between theorem and experiment

The information-theoretic impossibility principle is mathematical under its stipulated Markov assumptions. Every numerical effect of learning, model size, reward misalignment and environment shift is an empirical question. A small synthetic experiment may support or refute an instance of a hypothesis without proving it for every engine or environment.

Scholarly lineage

The study adapts experimental patterns from OpenAI's Let's Verify Step by Step (process/outcome verification), Weak-to-Strong Generalization, Why Language Models Hallucinate and PaperBench (rubrics), together with DeepMind's Chinchilla investigation (vary model/data independently) and the WILDS domain-shift methodology. It does not reproduce their model sizes, datasets, training regimes or published numerical outcomes.

Controls not yet satisfied

No frontier-scale model has been trained. Synthetic observations are not real-world evidence. Small-seed bootstrap intervals do not guarantee population coverage. The computational operation count is a proxy, not measured energy or FLOPs. CSV evaluations reveal correlations, not causal structure. Optional local-model arithmetic benchmarking has narrow coverage. The full method and sources are in RESEARCH_METHODS.md and SOURCES.md.
