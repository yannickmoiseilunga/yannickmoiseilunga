# Research integrity & scope

**Claims supported:**
- The attached records display controlled contrasts, exploratory transfer forecasts, source-wise failures and retained empirical predictions.
- Under indistinguishable observed evidence, universally correct point predictions can be impossible; this is an established inferential limitation.
- The conditional V3 law is mathematically well-formed and generated exploratory mean-error gains, not statistical confirmation.

**Claims NOT supported:**
- A newly proven universal law of artificial intelligence.
- Architecture-independent emergent ability, AGI, consciousness or causal universality from toy/benchmark data.
- Independent preregistration before development or third-party peer reproduction.
- That median-feature domain split equals a hospital/site/time shift.
- That 20 seeds represent 20 independently sampled environments.
- That local Ollama models were benchmarked in the reported tabular study.

**Potential source of bias:** researcher-guided redesign across versions; familiarity with published benchmarks; selection of conditions after observing early results; source-family count too small for precise transfer intervals. These risks are documented, not eliminated by the number of runs.

**Freeze/confirm:** a future confirmatory replication must freeze code, coefficients, domain eligibility, power calculation, tolerable effect size and interval method before inspecting independent labels. Publish all outcomes and provenance.

**Interpretations:** Newton's vocabulary is an explanatory analogy. The log-odds model follows a chosen exponential correction parameterisation, not Newton's second law. Statistical laws apply only under specified assumptions; no-free-lunch-type indistinguishability counterexamples rule out unconstrained universal point forecasting.
